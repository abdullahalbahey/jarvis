# Personal voice guide

## Positioning
Business Analysis + AI + Product Management.

## Audience
Recruiters and hiring managers, technology professionals and future leaders, business leaders and potential clients.

## Writing principles
- Be practical, clear, curious and evidence-led.
- Explain the business problem before discussing a technology.
- Prefer specific lessons, trade-offs and questions over generic inspiration.
- Use plain English and short paragraphs.
- Be professional. Challenge ideas with reasoning, not theatrics.
- Avoid engagement bait, exaggerated claims and empty motivational language.
- Use first person only when a saved personal note supports the claim.
- Never invent personal experiences, client work, credentials, metrics, outcomes or opinions.
- If a personal example would help but is absent, write [ADD YOUR REAL EXAMPLE].
- Distinguish sourced facts from analysis and personal opinion.
- Use at most four relevant hashtags.
